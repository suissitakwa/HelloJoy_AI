Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

HelloEver AI ("we", "us") provides an AI phone receptionist for appointment businesses such as dog groomers, hair salons, barbershops and nail salons. This policy explains what information we handle, why, who helps us process it, and the choices you have. Questions: takwa@helloeverai.com.

Two kinds of people we handle information for

Businesses that use HelloEver (our customers), and their callers and customers, who call or book with those businesses. For callers' information, the business is the one the caller is dealing with; we handle that information on the business's behalf, to run its receptionist.

What we collect

From Information
Business owners and staff Name, email, password (stored only as a secure hash), the business profile you give us (name, phone, services, prices, hours, policies, answers to common questions, documents you upload), and sign-in records.
Callers and customers of a business What a front desk would note: name, phone number or email, the pet's name for pet businesses, the service and appointment time, the staff member if one was requested, and the reason for a callback request. To limit repeated calls, we keep a one-way hash of the caller's number, not the number itself, unless the caller gives it for a booking or callback.
Calls The time, length and cost of each call, and which actions the receptionist took. Calls are not recorded. The conversation is processed live to understand and answer the caller. We don't store audio or transcripts; our phone and AI providers may keep a transcript or logs for a limited time to operate their services (see below).
Website visitors If you try the voice demo, your microphone audio is sent live to our AI provider to answer you; the transcript stays in your browser and is gone when you leave. Our hosting provider keeps standard server logs. We don't use advertising or tracking cookies.

How we use it

To answer calls, book, change and cancel appointments, take callback requests and notify the business; to run, secure, measure and improve the service; to bill businesses; and to contact business owners about their account. We don't sell personal information, use callers' information to market to them, or share it except as described here.

We may also disclose information if the law requires it, or to protect people's safety or our rights.

How long we keep it

Business and booking information is kept while the business uses HelloEver. When a business leaves, we delete its data within 30 days of its request, after exporting it if asked. Notification records are deleted after 30 days. Call records (time, length, cost) are kept as long as needed for billing and limits. Providers keep their own logs under their own policies.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete your information by emailing takwa@helloeverai.com. If you're a caller or customer of a business, you can also ask the business directly. We'll answer within 30 days. California residents have rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act where it applies, and we won't treat you differently for using them.

Security

Information is sent over encrypted connections, passwords are stored only as hashes, and access to business data is limited to that business's signed-in staff. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects you, we'll tell you as the law requires.

Children

HelloEver is not meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date above, and email business owners about important changes before they take effect.
